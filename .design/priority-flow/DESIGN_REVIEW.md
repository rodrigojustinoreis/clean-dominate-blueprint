## Summary

Arlington originally had weak visual hierarchy: a text-only hero, a small generic city image and long uninterrupted text. The final version uses an authentic deep-cleaning image in the hero, an answer-first section immediately below it and a two-image real-work gallery. Potomac now shows the full Capital Clean Care team with a restrained blurred backdrop that preserves every person rather than cropping the portrait.

## Must Fix — completed

1. Arlington lacked a relevant hero image. Added a real Capital Clean Care power-scrubbing photograph.
2. Two Arlington work photographs were stored sideways. Created corrected upright variants and referenced only those variants.
3. Potomac used a generic mopping photograph. Replaced it with the full team photograph and preserved the complete composition.
4. Responsive validation passed at 1280, 768 and 375 pixels: zero overflow, one H1, one main landmark and zero broken images.

## Should Fix — completed

1. Added a clearly labeled real-team/detail-work gallery to break up Arlington's long-form content.
2. Used consistent cards, radii, borders and shadows already present in the site design system.
3. Retained direct quote and telephone actions above the fold.

## What Works Well

- Real company photography increases trust without fabricating local project claims.
- The Arlington hero has strong contrast and an immediate service/location hierarchy.
- The Potomac team image establishes a premium, owner-operated company impression.
- Both pages reorganize cleanly at mobile and keep calls to action visible.
- Silver Spring Airbnb now uses an authentic turnover image in the hero, a direct answer-first passage and a clearer host handoff process. Responsive checks passed with one H1, one main landmark, zero broken images and zero horizontal overflow.
