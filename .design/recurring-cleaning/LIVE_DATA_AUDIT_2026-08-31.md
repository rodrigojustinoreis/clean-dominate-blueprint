## Primary diagnosis

The page is indexable and technically fast, but visibility is not converting into clicks. The largest ranking constraint is not page speed: it is intent dilution and cannibalization between the regional service page, local recurring-cleaning pages, city hubs, and older resource articles. For the Bethesda commercial query, Semrush showed unrelated guides outranking the dedicated local recurring-cleaning URL.

## Applied locally (not deployed)

- Preserved one regional H1 and self-referencing canonical.
- Added an explicit `main` landmark for accessibility and AI-agent navigation.
- Strengthened the answer-first definition with the exact recurring-maintenance concept.
- Reframed regional price/coverage copy so it matches Maryland, DC, and Northern Virginia intent.
- Prioritized links from the regional hub to dedicated Bethesda, Washington DC, Rockville, Silver Spring, and Arlington recurring-cleaning pages.
- Kept city-specific intent off the regional title/H1 to reduce additional cannibalization.

## Separate sitewide action required

Audit the 11 competing URLs by query and decide, URL by URL, whether to retain, retarget, canonicalize, noindex, or redirect. This must be evidence-led; no mass redirects or removals should be made from the page-level task.
