# Rockville location page — deep SEO audit

Audited URL: `https://capitalcleancare.com/locations/rockville-md`  
Audit date: 2026-08-22  
Scope: rendered HTML, source implementation, Semrush US database, live/cached SERP, competitor pages, internal architecture, schema, images, crawlability, local SEO, and AI-search readiness.

## Outcome

The page is now a dedicated **Rockville cleaning-services hub**. It targets `cleaning services rockville md` and routes narrower intent to the existing house-cleaning, recurring, deep-cleaning, move-out, apartment, and eco-friendly subpages. This resolves the page's previous mixed intent and reduces internal keyword cannibalization.

## SERP and competitor findings

The Semrush August 2026 SERP snapshot contains an AI Overview, local pack, directory results, pricing questions, and local providers. Competitors repeatedly surface these proof elements:

- Large and recent review profiles (approximately 120–267 reviews among prominent map/AI results)
- Precise service menus: standard, deep, move-in/out, apartment/condo
- Eco-friendly or health-focused positioning
- Years in business and family/local ownership
- Pricing and booking clarity
- Strong third-party corroboration from Yelp and other local sources

The page now answers service selection, local coverage, price drivers, trust, and booking intent early. Review volume and third-party mentions require off-page work and must not be fabricated on the website.

## Implemented changes

1. Replaced the ambiguous 72-character “All Services” title with a focused 52-character title.
2. Rewrote the meta description around the cleaning-services hub intent.
3. Added a direct local answer near the top of the page.
4. Added a six-service decision table with descriptive internal anchors.
5. Linked the Rockville 2026 cost guide contextually.
6. Added visible update timing and ZIP coverage.
7. Aligned Service schema name, service type, description, and `areaServed` with the hub.
8. Preserved a single H1 and a logical heading hierarchy.
9. Added explicit hero width/height to reduce layout-shift risk.
10. Preserved the canonical, robots, hreflang, Open Graph, Twitter, sitemap, and prerendering implementation.
